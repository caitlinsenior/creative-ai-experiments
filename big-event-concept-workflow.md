# AI-Assisted Event Concept Workflow

## The experiment

Thinking big! Exploring how AI can support the early stages of experiential and event design without replacing the designer’s judgement or creative direction.

## The problem

Event projects often move quickly from a loose brief into a large number of physical and digital applications. A lot of time can be lost exploring directions that don’t scale well once they reach signage, stage graphics, screens, social assets and environmental applications.

## What I’m testing

Using AI during the early concept phase to:

* rapidly explore visual territories
* test how an idea might translate into physical environments
* generate references around materiality, lighting and scale
* challenge an initial creative direction before committing to it
* identify which concepts have enough flexibility to become a wider design system

## My approach

**Brief → human creative direction → AI-assisted exploration → designer evaluation → design system → production**

## Why I’m interested in this

I’ve spent much of my career designing for global events, where a creative idea has to work everywhere from a social post to a stage several metres wide.

I’m interested in how AI can make that exploration phase faster while still keeping creative judgement, brand thinking and real-world production constraints firmly with the designer.
